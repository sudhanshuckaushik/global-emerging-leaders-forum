Across democracies, there is no shortage of conferences *about* democracy. What
is missing is a space for the people already in the room — elected officials,
party leaders, members of government — to think together, off the record.

## The demographic gap

The average age of a head of government globally is over 60. In most national
legislatures, power skews decades older than the populations they represent.
This is not simply a symbolic problem. It shapes which issues get prioritised,
which time horizons feel urgent, and which futures feel possible.

> When young people in power are connected to one another and to the best
> available thinking, the quality of democratic decisions improves.

## What the forum does differently

- **By invitation.** A small, curated cohort — not an open conference.
- **Off the record.** Candid conversation, no press, no performance.
- **Cross-border.** Peers from across the Global South and beyond.
